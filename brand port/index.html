    BEHIND THE POSTER / DEV NOTES
    1. NOTE 01 Why an expo

      PXL is games, art, collectibles, and animation. An expo with booths, cosplay, an exhibition, and new releases lets people try all of it in one trip, and lets fans show up as their favorite characters.

    2. NOTE 02 Why SM City Cabanatuan

      It’s a central, family-friendly mall in Nueva Ecija that kids, students, and parents already visit, so it fits our all-ages audience and is easy to reach.

    3. NOTE 03 Why this look

      “PIX L’S INTO W’S” runs huge in the background, a chunky 3D controller is the hero, and floating voxel blocks make it feel like stepping inside a game. The date and venue sit in the corners in Tiny5, so they’re the first thing you read.

    4. NOTE 04 Why the new colors

      PXL World gets its own special-edition look. Daylight blues and arcade purples make the event feel like a limited drop, while our peach and red still show up on the D-pad and the blocks to tie it back to the core brand.
