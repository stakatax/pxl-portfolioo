01 / THE ORIGINEST. 2026

OLD-SCHOOL SOUL. NEXT-LEVEL IDEAS.

The future
looks a little
pixelated.

We grew up with little squares that made us feel big things.

Founded in 2026, PXL brings the spirit of ’80s and ’90s pixel entertainment into today. We’re building a home for original illustrations, collectibles, 8-bit games, and short animated stories.

Our name starts with a pixel and thinks extra large. The raised XL is a reminder that small beginnings can unlock entire worlds.

THE QUEST

Make pixel entertainment accessible to all ages through games, art, collectibles, and animation—preserving its 8-bit roots while exploring what comes next.

THE NEXT LEVEL

Bring pixel media into more everyday experiences, from mainstream gaming and short-form stories to physical collectibles, connecting new audiences with a lasting creative culture.
